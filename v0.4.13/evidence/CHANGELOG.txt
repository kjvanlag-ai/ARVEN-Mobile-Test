RELIVOR v0.4.13 - 2026-10-03
Base: v0.4.12. Two targeted corrections.

BACKGROUND: Only the existing full-page backdrop draws the landscape. Current
Action's art TextureRect is hidden and has no landscape texture; fishing no longer
draws LAKE. The existing hand/rod/line/float/fish/ripples paint transparently using
the same centered COVER transform as the backdrop. The float maps onto lake water
in the source image on mobile and desktop. No images or animation tempo changed.
Idle uses content height. The natural fishing tail finishes its 15-second cycle,
then its transparent stage collapses back to compact idle. Job switching clears it.

QUEUE: schedule selects the first currently runnable entry, preserving all waiting
entries, duplicates and their relative order. Active work is not preempted. Its
saved queue_index identifies the exact entry across repeat, completion and restore;
complete removes only that index after natural stop. Legacy head-active saves
migrate to index zero. Full-inventory entries wait instead of silently disappearing.
Manual Stop remains paused. Manual queues retain priority over Auto. No jobs or
resources are inserted automatically. Existing partial crafting is unchanged.

WAITING UI: Queue cards label the true active index NOW and display concrete
waiting reasons for blocked entries. If none can run, Current Action shows Queue
waiting plus the reasons, with no fake progress. Stopped plans show Queue paused.

Recipes, balance, skills, inventory rendering, asset files and 15-second cycle
remain unchanged. Earlier builds retained. One versioned release/publication.
