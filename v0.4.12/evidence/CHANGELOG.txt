RELIVOR v0.4.12 - 2026-10-02
Base: v0.4.11. Only Queue UI and cumulative crafting progress corrected.
- Run | Queue | Info remain visible for regular jobs at all tested widths.
  Queue uses existing enqueue/schedule functions, independently of Auto and
  current material/cap constraints. Those are evaluated at dispatch.
- Recipe and Current Action share total recipe progress: completed contributions
  plus worked fraction of the reserved current contribution. Reservations do not
  count as completed work. Partial contributions, including 1 Wood, supported.
- Optional saved craft_paid/craft_batches maps preserve material reservations and
  completed contributions alongside existing active/suspended work. Legacy staged
  saves migrate without counting reserved materials as already processed.
- Recipe output/mastery/effects occur only after all requirements and work finish.
  Repeatable recipes reset only their own completed contribution for the next item.
Production Fishing Rod price remains 5 Wood. All data, prices, XP rates, action
base times, graphics, animation and other scripts unchanged. Compact layout retained.
One new exported web build; earlier version directories and archives retained.
