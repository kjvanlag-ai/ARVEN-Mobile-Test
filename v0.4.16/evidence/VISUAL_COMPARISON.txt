10. Overall: sunset, lake, wolf, tent, castle/waterfall, seated silhouette and right inventory match
    the requested composition. NOT a100% match. Actual gameplay has more text/controls and fewer
    decorative panels. No fake skill-tree/navigation/day-night/Explore buttons or duplicate lake.

ASSET LIMITS / WHAT WOULD NEED IMPROVEMENT
lake_background.png: reconstructed sun/castle/waterfall/vegetation differ; exact matching requires
repainting these landmarks from the approved original while retaining the clean background.
fisher-body.png and fisher-right-forearm.png: different cloak/boot/silhouette and limited articulation.
A natural full cast/reel requires matching upper-arm/hand poses or an articulated character rig.
Current implementation intentionally uses a calm small tug, not a fabricated full cast or catch sprite.
bobber.svg and ripple.svg: simpler than the painted reference; matching painted effects would improve them.
panel_frame.png/button.png: need richer matching ornaments. No original branded RELIVOR logo/font supplied.
Inventory PNGs are reconstructed illustrations, not pixel-identical cutouts.
Moss and Clay are not existing items, so their supplied files remain unused; no resources were added.
Stone art is mapped to existing Flint, raw_pelt to Boar Hide/Wolf Pelt. Both skins share this illustration.

No physical-phone/Safari testing. Browser mobile evidence uses Edge touch emulation.
